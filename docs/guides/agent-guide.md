# Agent guide

You use Pulse to learn each campaign before you take its calls: read short Briefing Cards, answer Drills, then pass Verbal Practice calls. When every Checkpoint and every Verbal Practice scenario is done, you are certified on that Track.

## Getting started

<a id="sign-in"></a>

### Sign in

**What it is.** Pulse is invite-only. You sign in with your LC Number and password, with a LogiCall Google account, or with the setup link or Cohort join code your supervisor or LogiCall gave you.

**When to use it.** Every time you open Pulse, and the first time you set up your account.

**How to:**

1. Open the Pulse sign-in page.
2. With an LC Number: type it (for example LC-1042) in **LC Number or Email**, type your **Password**, then select **Sign In**.
3. With a LogiCall Google account (@logicall.io or @logicall.live): select **Continue with Google**.
4. First time with an invite email: open the setup link, choose a password (at least 10 characters) and select **Create my account**, or select **Use my Google account**.
5. First time with a join code: select **Have a Team Join Code? Claim Access**, type the code (for example THS-7842), select **Continue**, fill in **Full Name**, **LC Number** and a password (at least 10 characters), then select **Create Account & Sign In**.
6. Given a temporary password (a new account from your supervisor or LogiCall, or after a reset): sign in with it, then type your own password twice on **Choose a new password** and select **Save password and continue**.

**Tips:**

- A Google account outside @logicall.io and @logicall.live is refused unless it was invited first.
- A first-time @logicall.io / @logicall.live Google sign-in starts as an Agent in the LogiCall Internal Cohort.
- Passwords need at least 10 characters.
- Setup links work once. If yours says **Invite not available**, ask for a new one.
- A deactivated account cannot sign in. Contact your LogiCall administrator.

<a id="forgot-password"></a>

### Forgot your password

**What it is.** How you get back in depends on how you sign in: LC Number agents get a temporary password from a person; email accounts get a reset link; Google accounts reset with Google.

**When to use it.** When your password does not work.

**How to:**

1. On the sign-in page, type your LC Number or email first, then select **Forgot password?**.
2. LC Number: the page tells you to ask your supervisor or LogiCall admin for a temporary password. They issue it from Pulse.
3. Email: select **Send reset link** and follow the link in your inbox.
4. Sign in with the temporary password. Pulse opens **Choose a new password**: type a new password (at least 10 characters) twice and select **Save password and continue**.

**Tips:**

- You cannot skip the new-password step: every page sends you back to it until you save.
- Google sign-in accounts have no Pulse password. Reset it with Google.

<a id="home"></a>

### Your Home page

**What it is.** Home shows your Tracks (one per campaign), your XP, streak and readiness, and how many Keep-Sharp reviews are due.

**When to use it.** Start every session here to see what to do next.

**How to:**

1. Look at **Assigned Tracks**. Tracks you have started come first, then new ones, then certified ones.
2. Select a Track card (**Start Track**, **Continue** or **Practice Again**) to open its Track Map.
3. If the **Knowledge Retention** card says reviews are due, select **Start Keep-Sharp Review**.

**Tips:**

- You see a Track when it is assigned to one of your Cohorts or to you by name, and it is in one of your Divisions (for example Telehealth). If a Track you expect is missing, ask your supervisor.
- The progress bar reaches 100% only after every Checkpoint and every Verbal Practice scenario is passed.

**Open in Pulse:** **Go to Home** (`/`)

## Learning a Track

<a id="track-map"></a>

### The Track Map

**What it is.** The Track Map is the path through one Track: numbered Checkpoints in order, then Verbal Practice at the end, plus your **Path to certification**.

**When to use it.** Each time you open a Track, to pick the next step.

**How to:**

1. From Home, select a Track card.
2. Select **Start Checkpoint** on the next open Checkpoint.
3. Finished Checkpoints show your first-try score and a **Practice Again** button.
4. When every Checkpoint is done, the **Verbal Practice** step at the end unlocks.

**Tips:**

- Checkpoints open strictly in order: "Complete Checkpoint 2 to unlock." means finish Checkpoint 2 first.
- The first-try score pill is green at 85% or more, amber at 70% or more.
- The **Path to certification** panel lists what is left: "Finish every Checkpoint" and "Pass every Verbal Practice scenario".

<a id="checkpoints"></a>

### Checkpoints: Briefing Cards and Drills

**What it is.** A Checkpoint teaches a few Playbook rules with Briefing Cards, then checks them with short Drills. You finish it by getting every Drill right; missed Drills come back until you clear them.

**When to use it.** To learn a campaign, one Checkpoint at a time. Most take a few minutes.

**How to:**

1. Read each Briefing Card: headline, **Key Rule**, and the **Do** / **Don't** lists. Select **Next** (or press → or Enter).
2. On the last card, select **Start Drills**.
3. Answer the Drill and select **Check** (or press Enter).
4. Read the feedback. If it says **Review Rule**, read the correct answer: that Drill comes back at the end for another try.
5. Select **Continue**, and on the last Drill **Finish Drills**.
6. On the **Checkpoint Complete** screen, select **Next Checkpoint**, or **Go to Verbal Practice** after the last one.

**Tips:**

- Drill types: **Scenario Choice**, **Spot the Violation**, **Call-Flow Order**, **Match Pairs** and **Complete the Script**. Number keys pick answers.
- Select **Source: Playbook p. N** (or press Space) to see where a rule comes from.
- XP: 15 for each first-try correct answer, 5 for a corrected one, plus 50 for finishing. XP is given only the first time; **Practice Again** gives no XP.
- To leave mid-way, select the X at the top left. A Checkpoint you leave starts from the beginning next time.

<a id="verbal-practice"></a>

### Verbal Practice

**What it is.** Verbal Practice is a practice call with an AI caller. You answer in your own words, typed or dictated, and get a scorecard on what you said and which Playbook rules you followed.

**When to use it.** After every Checkpoint in a Track is done. Passing every scenario is the last step to certification.

**How to:**

1. On the Track Map, select **Start** next to a Verbal Practice scenario.
2. Read the **Scenario brief** and the account on file, then select **Start Verbal Practice**.
3. Type your reply in the box (Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line), or select the mic to dictate and then send.
4. Keep going until the caller wraps up or you reach the turn limit.
5. Select **Finish & Grade Practice**. Grading can take up to a minute.
6. Read the scorecard: **Strengths**, **Focus Next Time** and **What to fix** (with "Try saying"). Select **Try again** if you did not pass.

**Tips:**

- You pass at the score shown ("Pass at 80%", for example). Breaking a critical rule is an **Automatic Fail**, whatever the score.
- If the mic is blocked, allow it in your browser's site settings, or just type. Typing always works.
- You can do up to 15 Verbal Practice sessions a day. Sessions you leave before your first reply do not count.
- If the AI coach is unavailable, your call is saved as "scorecard pending" and a Trainer can score it.
- If you leave and come back, select **Resume verbal practice** to pick up where you stopped.

<a id="certification"></a>

### Getting certified

**What it is.** You are certified on a Track when every Checkpoint is complete and every Verbal Practice scenario is passed on its current version.

**When to use it.** Check the **Path to certification** panel on the Track Map to see what is left.

**How to:**

1. Finish every Checkpoint in the Track.
2. Pass every Verbal Practice scenario.
3. The Track card on Home shows **Certified**, and the Track Map shows **Campaign Certified**.

**Tips:**

- Keep-Sharp reviews keep rules fresh but do not affect certification.
- **Recertify** means the Track changed in a way that needs a new pass. Your old certification stays in your history.
- **Revoked** means a Trainer withdrew your certification on that Track. The reason shows in **Path to certification**. It does not come back by itself, even if you redo the Track, so talk to your supervisor.
- Certification shows training is done. Your supervisor decides when you take live calls.

## Keeping sharp

<a id="keep-sharp-review"></a>

### Keep-Sharp Review

**What it is.** Short recall Drills on rules you already learned, spaced out at about 1, 3 and 7 days so they stick.

**When to use it.** Whenever Home or the Track Map says reviews are due. A few minutes a day keeps your streak going.

**How to:**

1. Select **Retention** in the sidebar, or **Start Keep-Sharp Review** on Home.
2. Select **Start Due Review**. With several Tracks, pick a Track pill first to review only that one.
3. Answer each Drill and select **Next Review Drill**, then **Finish Review**.

**Tips:**

- Nothing due? **Practice Upcoming Drills** lets you review early.
- A wrong answer comes back in about 4 hours.
- No reviews yet means you have not finished a Checkpoint. Reviews start after your first one.
- Reviews keep rules fresh. They do not count toward certification, and skipping them never removes it.
- **Mastered** (on the Keep-Sharp page; "fully retained" on Home) = rules you answered correctly in a spaced review and that are now on the longest (7-day) review gap.

**Open in Pulse:** **Open Keep-Sharp Review** (`/learn/review`)

<a id="readiness-passport"></a>

### My Readiness Passport

**What it is.** Your own record: XP, streak, certifications, skill radar, first-try Drill accuracy against Verbal Practice scores, and your graded practice transcripts with coaching.

**When to use it.** To see your strengths and where to focus, or to show your supervisor your progress.

**How to:**

1. On Home, select **My Readiness Passport**.
2. Scroll to **Verbal Practice transcripts** to reread a call and its scorecard.

**Tips:**

- Only you, your supervisor and LogiCall staff can see your Passport.

**Open in Pulse:** **Open my Passport**

<a id="account-menu"></a>

### Theme, How To and signing out

**What it is.** The account menu (your initials, top right) holds the color theme, a link to this How To page, and **Sign out**.

**When to use it.** When the screen is hard to read, when you need help, or on a shared computer.

**How to:**

1. Select your initials in the top-right corner.
2. Under **Theme**, pick **Light**, **Dark**, **Contrast** or **System**. Pulse remembers your choice.
3. Select **How To** to open this page. **How To** is also at the bottom of the left sidebar.
4. Select **Sign out** when you finish on a shared computer.

**Open in Pulse:** **Open How To** (`/how-to`)
