# 06 — Standard Meds Content Pack

## 1. Objective

Define the minimum trainer-approved curriculum and assessment coverage for all nine supplied Standard Meds PDFs. This is the content blueprint for implementation, not a license for the model to invent missing operational details.

Page references below are PDF page numbers. They identify source material for trainer review; they do not eliminate the claim-to-excerpt approval requirements in document 02.

Each track should contain these unit types as applicable:

1. orientation, customer state, and goal;
2. product or queue knowledge;
3. funnel/case branch recognition;
4. pre-call inspection;
5. dialogue and objection handling;
6. workflow actions and link/escalation assets;
7. compliance and privacy;
8. dispositions and wrap-up;
9. required scenario assessment.

The overview PDF supplies navigation and track relationships. It should produce a short orientation module and source-bundle checks, not artificial call scenarios.

## 3. Shared foundations pack

**Primary sources:** SM-01 pp. 14–15; cross-check SM-02 pp. 10–12. Track-specific additions remain in their own packs.

Required rules:

- self-identify as a Standard Meds caller and not a doctor;
- respond honestly if asked whether the caller is a robot;
- call only 09:00–21:00 in the lead’s local timezone;
- disclose recording where required;
- honor clear opt-out immediately and flag permanent suppression;
- do not cross-sell another product on a recovery call;
- use no invented discount, coupon, or unapproved arrangement;
- do not claim approval or prescription readiness before physician review;
- do not diagnose, cure-claim, or create false urgency;
- do not invoke an SMS link-send to voicemail;
- respect approved offer/re-offer limits;
- send only the correct approved link after the required consent/state.

Minimum learning evidence:

- server-scored knowledge items for each critical rule;
- varied retrieval across at least two products;
- outbound local-time and opt-out action cases;
- voicemail SMS prohibition;
- premature approval and medical-boundary cases in product-specific contexts.
